I can barely touch my toes. Is this course too advanced for me?

+

Not at all.
About 80% of our students start exactly where you are — stiff, tight, and unsure where to begin. The program is built to:
• adapt to your current level
• progress gradually and safely
• help you feel stronger and more mobile step by step
You don’t need to be flexible to start — flexibility comes from the process.

How can I watch the workouts on my TV?

+

You can easily stream the workouts using one of these options:
1. Open the platform directly through your Smart TV browser
2. Use your phone or laptop + Smart TV with Cast or Screen Mirroring
3. Connect your device to the TV using an HDMI cable
You can also train from a PC or laptop — just open the platform link sent to your email.

Do I need special equipment to participate?

+

No gym equipment is required. For the best experience, we recommend:
• Comfortable workout clothes
• An exercise or yoga mat
• Yoga blocks and a foam roller (helpful but not mandatory)
All exercises are demonstrated with modifications.

I don’t have yoga blocks or a roller. What can I use instead?

+

You can easily replace them with household items:

Instead of yoga blocks:
• Thick, stable books
• Shoeboxes or sturdy boxes
• Stacks of folded towels

Instead of a foam roller:
• A tightly rolled towel
• A firm water bottle
• A massage ball or tennis ball

The goal is support and comfort — not perfection.

Can I join if I’m pregnant?

+

If you’re pregnant, we strongly recommend consulting your doctor before starting. While many exercises are gentle, it’s important to make sure everything is safe and suitable for your individual situation.

What if I have chronic conditions or had surgery?

+

If you have chronic health conditions or recently underwent surgery, please consult a healthcare professional before beginning. Exercises should feel controlled and comfortable, never painful.

How can I get the best results?

+

For the best progress:
• Follow Karyna’s guidance closely

• Support your body with proper rest and nutrition
Consistency matters more than intensity.
